Bible Verse Widget – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

In short: Bible Verse Widget has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and it does not send anything to the developer. Your settings and saved verses stay on your phone. The app connects to the internet only to download Bible verse text.

Who is responsible for the app

Bible Verse Widget ("the app") is published by Collin Fox ("the developer"). Questions about this policy can be sent to collinj.fox1@gmail.com.

What the app stores on your phone

The app keeps the following on your device only. It is never uploaded by the app:

  • Your choices in the app's settings, such as the Bible translation, widget text size, widget background and how often the verse changes.
  • The verse currently shown on the widget, and the verses you saved.
  • Times the app uses to schedule and time its work, such as when the last verse was downloaded and when the app was last opened.

This information is removed when you uninstall the app.

Diagnostic log file

The app writes a plain-text diagnostic log to your phone's Documents folder (a file named like psalm_log_….txt). It records what the app did and why something may have failed, for example times, which translation and passage were requested, error messages, and device conditions that affect background work such as Battery Saver, Data Saver and whether a network was available. The app never sends this file anywhere. You can open or delete it at any time with a file manager. The file may remain on your phone after the app is uninstalled until you delete it. If you choose to send the file to the developer, that is your decision.

Internet use

To show a verse, the app downloads Bible text from these services:

  • bible-api.com
  • query.getbible.net (getBible)
  • cdn.jsdelivr.net (jsDelivr)
  • raw.githubusercontent.com (GitHub)

Each request contains the translation, book, chapter and verse numbers needed to get the text. It does not include your name, an account, saved verses or any other personal information from the app. Like any internet request, it reveals standard technical information to the service that receives it, such as your IP address and basic device and software details. These services are run by other parties and have their own privacy practices. The developer does not operate them and does not receive this information.

Permissions

  • Internet – to download Bible verse text, as described above.
  • Foreground service – to show a brief notification while the app refreshes the verse on the widget, so Android lets the refresh finish.

The app does not ask for access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or files.

What the app does not do

  • It has no user accounts or sign-in.
  • It shows no ads.
  • It contains no analytics, advertising or tracking libraries, and it does not sell or share personal information.

If you installed the app from Google Play, Google may provide the developer with aggregated crash and performance reports for users who opted in to share them with Google. The app does not collect or send these itself.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the app's handling of information changes, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change.
